Projects

Selected research and development projects spanning medical imaging, biomedical signal processing, applied artificial intelligence, real-time deployment, and remote sensing.

Overview

My projects focus on translating AI and signal processing methods into practical tools for health and biomedical applications. This includes ultrasound guidance, Doppler and echocardiography analysis, voice and breathing-based monitoring, smartphone and real-time AI deployment, and earlier work in multispectral image fusion and remote sensing.

Featured Research Projects

AI-Guided Ultrasound and Operator Support

Developing machine learning tools that help guide ultrasound acquisition, support untrained or minimally trained users, and improve standardization of image capture in challenging clinical or austere environments.

Ultrasound AI Operator Guidance Clinical Translation

Automated Venous Gas Emboli Detection

Building deep learning systems for automatic grading and detection of venous gas emboli from Doppler ultrasound audio and echocardiographic data, including real-time feasibility and software deployment.

Doppler Ultrasound Deep Learning Real-Time AI

Speech and Audio Biomarkers for Health

Investigating voice, breathing, and acoustic biomarkers for health monitoring, including respiratory stress, edema-related signatures, and speech enhancement for medical applications.

Audio AI Voice Biomarkers Digital Health

Medical Imaging AI

Designing AI pipelines for quantitative analysis of ultrasound and CT data, including applications in pleural effusions, endometriosis staging, diabetic pathology, and image-based decision support.

Medical Imaging Quantification Decision Support

Smartphone and Edge Deployment

Translating trained machine learning models into practical, real-time systems on smartphones and portable devices for accessible health technologies and point-of-care use.

Smartphone AI Edge Computing Real-Time Systems

Multispectral Image Fusion and Remote Sensing

Earlier work focused on deep learning and optimization-based methods for multispectral image fusion, pansharpening, and remote sensing image analysis, forming the basis of my doctoral research.

Image Fusion Remote Sensing Deep Learning
