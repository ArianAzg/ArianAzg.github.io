About

Welcome to my research website. I am a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Radiology at UNC Chapel Hill focused on developing practical and clinically meaningful AI methods for health applications. My work spans multimodal biomedical data, diagnostic support systems, medical imaging, and signal-based analytics with an emphasis on the deployment in clinical systems.

Research

Applied AI for Health

Designing robust machine learning and deep learning tools that address real clinical and healthcare challenges.

Medical Imaging

Developing image-based AI pipelines for ultrasound and other imaging modalities to support guided acquisition, and real-time early screening workflows.

Biomedical Signals

Analyzing physiological and acoustic signals including voice biomarkers to improve detection of health-related conditions.
